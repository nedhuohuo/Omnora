# Omnora commercial licensing

Omnora is planned as a dual-licensed project. Until a legal commercial licensor is designated, only the AGPL community option is available.

## Community option

The source code in this repository is available under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`), as provided in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Application notices for Omnora must use `AGPL-3.0-only` and must not offer “or any later version.”

The AGPL permits personal, educational, nonprofit, and commercial use subject to its terms. In particular, distribution and certain network use of modified versions may require corresponding source code to be offered under the AGPL.

## Proprietary commercial option

An organization that wants to use, modify, integrate, distribute, or provide Omnora under terms that do not include the AGPL obligations must obtain a separate written commercial license from a designated Omnora Project legal licensor.

A commercial agreement may define proprietary-use rights, support, warranties, indemnities, service levels, redistribution rights, and other negotiated terms.

This document is a licensing policy notice. It does not itself grant a commercial license, waive any AGPL requirement, quote pricing, or create a support commitment.

## Third-party software

A commercial license for Omnora does not replace or override licenses for third-party dependencies. Every distribution must continue to comply with those licenses and notices.

## Current availability

No legal commercial licensor or public commercial contact has been designated yet. Until one is designated, only the AGPL community option is available. There is currently no commercial license to purchase or request.

This document is not legal advice. The final dual-license program and commercial agreement should be reviewed by qualified counsel before commercial licensing begins.
